Questions, answered straight.

Does it really work with my laser?

If your machine takes SVG, DXF, or GCode, yes. Magic Engraver exports laser-ready files with suggested power/speed settings per material, tuned for popular machines from xTool, OmTech, Creality, Thunder, Epilog, and generic K40s — plus native support on Glowforge hardware.

What exactly is gated behind the account?

Trying the tool is free and anonymous. Seeing your finished engraving preview — and exporting the laser-ready file — requires a free Glowforge account, which starts your 14-day Premium trial. No credit card.

What happens after the 14 days?

Your designs stay yours. Magic Engraver and other Premium tools require a subscription after the trial; we'll tell you before anything changes, and there's no auto-charge because we never took a card.

Is my photo private?

Yes. Uploaded images are used only to generate your design, are never used to train models, and can be deleted from your account at any time.
